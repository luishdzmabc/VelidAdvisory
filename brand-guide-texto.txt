01 BRAND OVERVIEW



Who VeliD Advisory is

VeliD Advisory is Carin Verbree’s strategy and sustainability advisory. It helps
ambitious SMEs grow across borders, starting between the Netherlands and
Mexico, with Southern France and Spain next.

The name
VeliD combines Carin’s surname, Verbree, with the first names of her two children. It is a
personal name, not a category label, and that is deliberate. The firm is built on twenty years of
lived experience, not on a generic formula that could belong to anyone.


The tagline
Growth that lasts. In full: Helping companies build international growth that lasts. It says what
the firm is for in plain words, without borrowing the language of compliance or the vagueness
of “making the world better.”

“I don’t believe growth and sustainability are separate questions.”

 KEY TAKEAWAY
 VeliD Advisory exists because international growth built without a sustainability lens rarely
 holds. The Holland House Mexico track record proves that, so the brand doesn’t need to declare
 it.




02 POSITIONING



How VeliD Advisory positions itself

Nineteen years in Mexico, seven and a half of them leading the Dutch-
Mexican Chamber of Commerce, taught Carin what makes international
growth work and what quietly breaks it.

She has seen companies of every size succeed and fail abroad, which makes it easy to spot the
common mistakes early: the wrong partner, an offer that was never adapted to the local
market, a positioning that simply didn’t land. She also knows that growth only holds if it is
financially, socially, environmentally and organizationally future-proof, and she brings a wide
network of professionals and organizations, built over two decades, into every project.

03 VOICE & TONE



How VeliD Advisory sounds

The voice is warm, concrete and grounded in what actually happened, not
in how impressive it can be made to sound.

In practice
Speak in the first person when describing experience and proof; Carin lived this, so the writing
says “I,” not a distanced “VeliD Advisory” hiding the person behind it. Use “VeliD Advisory”
when describing the offer itself, especially anywhere the firm may one day include other
people. Every claim about the track record has to be accurate and checkable. If a number or
an outcome can’t be confirmed, it doesn’t go in.


House rules
• No em dashes, ever, anywhere.

• No declarative self-promotion. Don’t say “I know these markets”; show what was built and
  let the reader conclude it.

• Avoid short, punchy sentences that read like they were generated to sound confident. Let
  sentences breathe and explain.

• Keep the register hands-on and specific: cultural training, market entry, a storyline,
  topicalization, not “leveraging synergies.”

 KEY TAKEAWAY
 Say what’s true, in a way that shows it happened. The brand doesn’t need to declare its own
 quality.

04 LOGO



The mark

The primary logo is the full VeliD Advisory wordmark. The V and D carry the
brand’s two accent colors; the underscore after the D is part of the mark, not
punctuation.




For small spaces (favicons, social avatars, watermarks) use the icon-only mark: the colored V-D
monogram without the word “Advisory” beneath it.


On dark backgrounds
Use the reversed lockup, cream wordmark with the same coral and teal accents, on Navy.
Never place the standard dark-text logo on a dark background, and never place the reversed
light logo on a light background.




Rules
• Keep clear space around the mark at least the height of the “V.”

• Never stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects such as shadows or outlines to the logo.

• Don’t place other text or graphics inside the clear space.

• The icon-only mark may stand alone. Don’t crop the full wordmark to fit a small space; use
  the icon instead.

06 TYPOGRAPHY



Typography

Two typefaces carry the whole brand. Poppins for anything that leads,
Montserrat for anything that explains.

Poppins SemiBold or Bold sets titles, headings, labels and short callouts, in Navy or Teal
depending on the level. Montserrat Regular carries every paragraph of running text, lead
paragraphs and captions, mostly in dark grey or muted tones, so Navy stays reserved for
structure, not decoration.
The exact style-by-style breakdown is on the last page of this guide (VeliD Style Map). Apply styles from the Word
Styles pane; don’t set fonts and colors by hand.

07 TRACK RECORD



Experience you can measure

Carin took over a network of 17 member companies at Holland House
Mexico. When she left, it had grown to 120+ members and partners, and
turnover had increased from 2.5 million to 11.4 million pesos.

She transformed a network originally built around oil and gas into a platform for sustainable
trade. Membership naturally comes and goes over the years, but not one member left
specifically because of that shift. That matters more than the numbers on their own: the
change held because it was built with people, not imposed on them.

 KEY TAKEAWAY
 This is the proof that carries the brand. Lead with it wherever the audience needs to trust Carin
 before the first conversation, not with adjectives about how experienced she is.




08 SERVICES & WAY OF WORKING



What VeliD Advisory does

Apart from a relatively standard diagnostic report, there is no fixed package,
because every company that comes to VeliD is in a different place. The
shape of the work depends on what is actually needed, the stage the
company is in, and the budget.

Areas of expertise
• Market entry: strategy and practical groundwork for entering a new country.

• Strategy and offer repositioning, including topicalization: adapting a product, service or
  story so it actually lands in a new market instead of arriving as a direct translation of what
  worked at home.

• Sustainability as a competitive advantage, not a compliance exercise.

• Cultural training, team workshops and speaking sessions on international business and
  cross-cultural collaboration.

• Network and introductions, built over two decades, deployed for the client instead of
  starting cold.

09 NETWORK & COLLABORATION



How the network works

VeliD Advisory is built on a network in Mexico and beyond, from institutions
to individual specialists, connected through Netherlands Chambers
International to Chambers of Commerce worldwide.

Independent collaborators bring specific expertise into a project when it calls for it. Each
invoices the client directly and is introduced by name and specialism. They are not presented
as VeliD team members, both because that is what the current legal structure requires and
because it keeps the positioning honest: VeliD is Carin’s practice, supported by a network, not
a firm with staff it doesn’t have.

 KEY TAKEAWAY
 Never write “our team” or “we” in a way that implies employees. Write “VeliD Advisory works
 with [name], who specializes in [x]” instead.
